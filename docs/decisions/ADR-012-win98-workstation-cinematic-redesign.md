## Date
2026-07-18

## Context

The owner is redesigning the portfolio from scratch (branch `redesign-attempt2`) around
one autobiographical image: **himself at his literal first computer — a Windows 98
machine — and the portfolio living *inside* that computer**, the CRT monitor acting as
the site's UI. Target quality bar: Awwwards/FWA-tier cinematic coherence. The full
requirements interview (2026-07-18, ten questions, all resolved) is summarised in the
Decision sections below; each § records a decision that was explicitly made, not a
default.

Constraints that shaped everything:

- **No DCC tooling.** Blender is not installed and will not be. Whatever exists must be
  generated by code at runtime. This kills any photoreal ambition honestly (a
  code-generated realistic human lands in the uncanny valley) and makes "procedural" a
  literal description of the pipeline, not a mood word.
- **The repo already provides the hard parts of a stack**: three.js 0.185, React Three
  Fiber 9, drei, `postprocessing`, GSAP 3.15, Lenis, Next 16 static export, Tailwind 4 —
  plus seeded RNG (`src/lib/prng.ts`) and a runtime FPS watchdog pattern
  (`src/lib/gpuTier.ts`). No new runtime dependencies are anticipated.
- **All portfolio content already exists** in `src/lib/*.ts` (5 projects with
  problem/approach/outcome/`howAI`, animatable SVG diagrams, fabricated screenshots with
  fictional domains; a 5-role experience timeline; tiered skills; stats; social links)
  and `public/resume.pdf`. The redesign re-houses content; it does not rewrite it.
- **Reference assets** live in `assets-src/workstation/`: three AI-generated concept
  sheets (the "model stats"/"4K maps" panels in them are fictional decoration — no real
  3D asset exists) establishing the scene, plus `tattoo01–04.jpg`, the owner's actual
  right-forearm tattoos. `assets-src/` is source material only and is never shipped.

## Decision

### 1. Story and voice — origin story, told by the machine
The experience is an **origin story leading, with the machine's own playful
personality as the voice** (interview Q5: "A leading with C's mood"). The Win98 box is
the owner's actual first computer; the visitor arrives in that memory and discovers
2026-grade work running inside a 1998 machine. Comedy is seasoning (dialog boxes with
personality, gags in §6), sincerity is the skeleton. Target exit state: *charmed +
"this person has been building things forever"* → contact.

### 2. Art direction — stylized full color on clay-clean forms, at dusk
- **Character:** semi-stylized, built as if for a clay render (clean forms first),
  then colored. The face is **never the hero** — no close-up face realism is attempted,
  ever. Signature features must read instantly at mid-shot: short curly hair, full
  beard, hoop earring (left), earbuds, black tee, jeans, smartwatch, and the
  right-forearm tattoos (§3).
- **Lighting:** **dusk** (owner's explicit call — night reads too dark). Cool blue-hour
  ambient fill from a window separates the figure from the room; the **CRT is the warm
  key light** and the literal light of the scene — the portfolio illuminates its
  author. The screen's emissive output drives a real light whose intensity/color
  follows screen content (boot flicker, BSOD blue, shutdown amber).
- **Garnish (tiered, sheddable):** scanline crawl + phosphor flicker + bloom on the
  CRT; cast screen-light flicker on figure and desk; dust motes in a faked window
  light-shaft (billboard rays — **no true volumetrics, no fog, no cloth/cable
  physics**; faked versions read identically at our camera distances and were chosen
  deliberately).

### 3. Pipeline — 100 % runtime-procedural three.js
Everything is code-built at runtime: seeded (`prng.ts` `mulberry32`), parametric,
version-controlled as TypeScript. No GLB imports, no baked textures from DCC tools.

- **Character:** primitive/lathe/curve construction; hair as instanced tube-curls;
  beard as a shaped volume with surface break-up; hands simplified (they rest on the
  keyboard; finger taps are the detail that sells them, not knuckle topology).
- **Textures** are generated into canvases at startup (wood grain, aged plastic,
  paper, skin tone zones) and uploaded once. The **tattoos ship as painted, simplified
  recreations** in the arm albedo — inner-wrist glyph stack, ninja-star, molecule
  diagram, pocket-watch + roses, outer-forearm feather/wing, and the red-and-black koi
  with lotus (the koi's red is the single strongest color accent on the figure and is
  placed to catch the CRT light pool). **The reference photos themselves never enter
  `public/` or the bundle.**
- **Props** (CRT, tower, keyboard, mouse, speaker, mug "Ctrl Alt Del", spiral
  notebook + pen, CD jewel-case stack, floppy disks, cables, desk, chair, wall poster,
  room shell) are hard-surface procedural geometry — the cheap, safe half of the
  pipeline.

### 4. Screen architecture — hybrid render-target / live DOM, with a dock contract
The monitor UI is the most consequential architecture decision (interview Q4):

- **Cinematic mode** (camera moving): the Win98 desktop renders into an offscreen
  buffer mapped onto the CRT glass with a CRT shader (barrel curvature, scanlines,
  phosphor mask, flicker, bloom). The buffer also feeds the screen-light (§2).
- **Docked mode**: when the story pushes the camera square-on into the monitor
  (chapter 4), the experience **cross-fades to pixel-aligned live React DOM** over the
  CRT — crisp text, real focus/tab/screen-reader semantics, draggable windows as divs.
  CSS carries the CRT look across the swap (scanline overlay, vignette, faked edge
  curvature) so the transition is invisible.
- **The dock contract:** *scroll is suspended while docked.* Navigation inside Windows
  is click-driven. Closing/minimizing everything surfaces an on-screen hint; scrolling
  resumes the camera journey. The swap moment (alignment, color match) is treated as a
  hard, explicitly-budgeted problem in the implementation plan, not an afterthought.

### 5. Chapters and camera — one vertical scrub timeline, two bookend gestures
Vertical scroll only, Lenis-smoothed, GSAP-ScrollTrigger-driven camera **scrub** with
soft snap at chapter rest points. ~60–90 s of scroll before the dock; the dock itself
is unbounded. Keyboard: arrow/space advance chapters (§9).

| # | Chapter | Camera | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | POWER ON | Black → power-button close-up | **Entry gesture:** site opens dark with the tower's power button glowing — *the visitor presses power*. That click unlocks audio (§7), lights the LED, degauss thunk, **BIOS POST counts the owner's real stats as hardware** (`Memory: 600+ client accounts OK`, `Detecting drives: 44 GMC sub-accounts found`), Win98-style splash, desktop settles. **Auto-plays (~5 s), skippable**; scrubbing a boot backwards feels wrong (owner-confirmed) |
| 1 | THE GLOW | Extreme CRT close-up, slow pull-back | Phosphor triads, scanline crawl, desktop through curved glass; name + role typed as if into the machine |
| 2 | THE MAN | Orbit right around the monitor edge | Reveal: the owner in profile, typing. Earbud, beard, tattooed forearm in the screen's light pool. Idle animation carries the chapter |
| 3 | THE ROOM | Slow dolly back + crane up | Dusk reveal: props, window light, cables, Win98 wall poster. Wide establishing shot — *this is where it started* |
| 4 | THE DOCK | Arc over the shoulder, push in until the screen fills the frame | Texture→DOM cross-fade; **scroll suspends**; visitor explores Windows freely (§6) |
| 5 | SIGN-OFF | Pull back to widest; dusk has deepened | Shut Down… → "It's now safe to turn off your computer" glows amber → final DOM layer: email, LinkedIn, GitHub, resume download. The figure leans back, stretches; the power LED stays on. *He's still building* |

Contact appears **twice by design**: playfully inside (Outlook, §6) and unmissably at
the end. Pacing reference: slow and contemplative (*Halt and Catch Fire* titles, the
warmth of *Her*); the owner named no further references.

### 6. The desktop metaphor map (all rows owner-approved)

| Content (source) | Win98 surface |
|---|---|
| Stats (`stats.ts`) | **BIOS POST** counts them as hardware during boot (§5 ch. 0) |
| Projects (`projects.ts`, 5) | `My Projects` desktop folder → Explorer → each project its own window; screenshots framed in **Internet Explorer** with the project's fictional `domain` in the address bar (period-correct successor to the Safari frame); `status: "in-progress"` renders as an unfinished-copy dialog for `personas` |
| Project diagrams (`public/diagrams/*.svg`) | The existing one-shot animated SVGs play inside the project window (ADR-002 pipeline reused) |
| Resume / Experience (`resume.ts`, `public/resume.pdf`) | `resume.doc` in a WordPad-style viewer with a `Download…` button serving the real PDF; the 5-role timeline as a **`C:\Career\` folder tree** (one folder per employer, oldest nested deepest) |
| Skills (`SKILL_TIERS`) | **Add/Remove Programs** — each tier a category, install dates riffing on when each skill was learned |
| About | `ABOUT_ME.txt` in Notepad, on the desktop |
| Contact (`nav.ts` SITE/SOCIAL) | **Outlook Express** compose window pre-addressed to the real email; LinkedIn/GitHub as desktop shortcuts that "dial up" |
| Easter eggs | ① Recycle Bin holds "old portfolio designs" (a nod to the retired Lens and the deleted redesign), ② **BSOD** on forbidden actions (deleting `System32`), recovers with a wink, ③ playable **Minesweeper**, ④ the desk's CD stack labeled with the owner's certifications |

MS Paint and DOS were considered and deferred to the easter-egg tier to protect scope.
The shell itself (taskbar, Start menu, window chrome, context menus) is one shared
component system all of the above render through.

### 7. Sound — earned by the power press, on by default, three tiers
The §5 power press is the browser-required user gesture: audio unlocks the moment the
machine turns on — no consent banner, no muted autoplay.

- **Tier 1 (always ship):** degauss thunk, BIOS beep, startup chime, mouse click,
  window open/close, error ding, shutdown sound, low CRT hum bed.
- **Tier 2 (texture):** keyboard clacks synced to idle typing, HDD seek chatter during
  boot, hum shift with screen brightness, egg stingers (BSOD, Minesweeper, Recycle Bin).
- **Tier 3 (room tone):** dusk ambience, fan whirr, faint music leaking from the
  earbuds when the camera orbits close to his head.

Persistent tiny mute toggle; state in `localStorage`. Static-floor visitors get no
audio bed. **No voice anywhere.** All sounds are original/openly-licensed *evocations*
of the era — see §10 for why nothing is sampled from Windows.

### 8. Performance — 60 fps desktop, 30 fps mobile floor, everything sheddable
- Targets: **60 fps desktop, 30 fps floor on mid-range mobile**, dynamic resolution
  scaling as the first-response lever.
- The `gpuTier.ts` pattern is reused: fidelity starts high; a runtime FPS watchdog
  **asks before downgrading** (ADR-010 §2's opt-in principle survives the Lens);
  `?tier=` override retained for QA.
- Effects are tiered for shedding, in order: dust/rays → cast-light flicker →
  bloom/CRT shader richness → idle-animation density → resolution. Geometry LOD is
  parametric (the procedural builders take a detail parameter).
- Loading: the boot sequence (§5 ch. 0) **is** the loader — POST lines double as real
  progress; heavy construction (character build, texture canvas bakes) happens behind
  it. Code-splitting keeps the docked Windows apps (IE, WordPad, Minesweeper…) out of
  the initial bundle; each app lazy-loads on first open with a period-appropriate
  hourglass.

### 9. Mobile and accessibility — adapted experience plus a static floor
- **Mobile (B):** same cinematic chapters with tuned-down effects and DRS; when
  docked, **windows open maximized, one at a time, full-screen** with large touch
  targets and swipe-to-close (authentic Win98 pattern, redesigned for thumbs).
- **Static floor (C):** `prefers-reduced-motion`, WebGL failure, or watchdog-declined
  weak GPUs get a clean, fast, fully readable 2D page carrying all content — this is
  simultaneously the accessibility fallback and the SEO surface.
- In the 3D experience: keyboard advances chapters; the docked UI is real DOM, so
  focus order, tab, and screen readers work natively. The `<canvas>` carries an
  `aria-hidden` cinematic and the content is never *only* inside it.

### 10. What retires, what survives, and the rules that bind the theme
- **Retired on this branch:** `src/components/lens/` (all of it — prism, refraction
  pass, kinetic registry, choreography), Lens-specific state and docs references.
  Deleted, not orbited around; git history is the archive.
- **Survives:** the content model (`src/lib/*.ts` — components read it exactly as
  before), `public/diagrams/` + `InlineDiagram`, `public/screens/` recreations,
  `public/resume.pdf`, `prng.ts`, the `gpuTier.ts` watchdog pattern, design-token
  discipline in `globals.css` (a new Win98-era token set is added; semantic classes,
  no raw hex).
- **Content rules restated as theme-independent:** no raw client screenshots — all
  imagery under `public/screens/` remains fabricated dummy-data recreations; client
  names and client financial figures appear nowhere in the repo.
- **IP rule for the Win98 homage:** ship **zero Microsoft assets**. All icons are
  original pixel-art in the period style; the boot splash, chime, and system sounds
  are original evocations, not samples; UI fonts are openly-licensed period-style
  faces, not Microsoft's; the visual language (grey chrome, bevels, teal desktop) is
  used as homage without reproducing trademarked marks — the concept sheets' literal
  Windows logo/wordmark do **not** ship. Minesweeper is an original implementation
  with original art.

## Architecture

- **Scene graph:** `WorkstationScene` root → `Room` (walls/floor/window light) →
  `Desk` group (props as independent procedural builders) → `Machine` (tower, CRT
  with screen mesh + light rig) → `Character` (parametric figure + idle-animation
  driver) → `Atmosphere` (dust, light shafts). Each builder is a pure
  seeded function returning geometry/materials, unit-testable without a canvas.
- **Screen state:** one `Win98State` store (desktop icons, z-ordered windows, focus,
  boot phase) consumed by *both* renderers — the render-target painter in cinematic
  mode and the live DOM in docked mode — so the swap is a view change, not a state
  handoff.
- **Choreography:** a single `Choreography` owner maps Lenis scroll → GSAP timeline →
  camera + lighting + chapter state, mirroring the Lens rule that frame loops read
  mutable state, never React state. React-compiler lint constraints carry over
  (seeded RNG only, uniform writes via refs).
- **Folder sketch:** `src/components/workstation/{scene,builders,character,crt,
  choreography}` + `src/components/win98/{shell,apps}` + `src/lib/win98State.ts`,
  `src/lib/chapters.ts`, `src/lib/audio.ts`. The static floor lives beside the
  experience as plain sections, not inside it.

## Alternatives Considered

- **DCC-authored character (Blender-scripted, baked GLB)** — the higher quality
  ceiling and the recommended path; **rejected by constraint**: no Blender, and the
  owner explicitly chose the pure-runtime pipeline (interview Q2).
- **Photoreal target ("Unreal cinematic / not stylized", the original brief)** —
  **rejected** as unreachable without DCC tooling; a near-miss human is worse than a
  stylized one (uncanny valley). The owner traded ultra-realism for guaranteed
  signature-feature likeness (Q1/Q3).
- **Texture-only screen** — perfect glass integration but blurry case-study text,
  zero accessibility, every widget re-implemented against raycast math; **rejected**.
- **DOM-only screen** — crisp but breaks the cinematic illusion (no curvature/bloom,
  z-order cracks during camera moves); **rejected**. The hybrid (§4) takes both costs
  where each is cheapest.
- **Scroll continues inside the desktop** — **rejected** for the dock contract (Q4):
  mixed scroll-and-click inside a windowed OS is ambiguous and fights both idioms.
- **Full-night lighting** — moodier but the owner judged it too dark; **dusk** keeps
  a legible figure and a color story (cool fill vs. warm key).
- **Full 3D experience on mobile (A)** and **desktop-only with mobile fallback (C
  alone)** — A founders on touch-hostile Win98 chrome and thermals; C alone loses the
  wow exactly where first impressions happen (LinkedIn in-app opens). The adapted
  B + C-floor pairing was chosen (Q8).
- **Voice-over** — considered, **rejected**: fights the machine-as-narrator voice and
  adds localization/quality risk for no structural gain.
- **MS Paint / DOS apps at launch** — deferred to easter-egg tier; each is real UI
  surface area and the six shipped apps already cover every content section.

## Consequences

### Positive
- One coherent, ownable concept: the portfolio is literally *inside* the owner's first
  computer, and the machine itself tells the story. Every section maps to a diegetic
  surface — no generic "sections" remain.
- The pipeline is fully deterministic and reviewable: geometry, textures, and
  animation are seeded TypeScript. No binary assets to version except audio files.
- Content model untouched — future content edits stay `src/lib/*.ts` edits, exactly
  as the repo's conventions promise.
- The hybrid screen gives crisp, accessible, indexable content without sacrificing
  the CRT-glass cinematography; the static floor doubles as SEO insurance.
- The boot-as-loader collapses the classic award-site preloader problem into the
  story's first beat.

### Negative / risks (accepted with eyes open)
- **The procedural character is the single biggest quality risk.** Code-built organic
  forms have a hard ceiling; if the figure underdelivers, the mitigations are already
  designed in (dusk rim-lighting, side/back framing, clay-first discipline) — but a
  weak silhouette would wound the whole concept. This is the first thing to prototype
  and the first owner-QA gate in the implementation plan.
- **The dock swap is a precision problem** (projection alignment, color/tone match
  across shader vs. CSS). Budgeted as its own slice with explicit acceptance criteria,
  not folded into "build the UI".
- **The Win98 shell is a lot of UI** (window manager, six apps, boot/shutdown, eggs).
  Scope is defended by the shared-chrome component system and by everything outside
  the six apps living in the easter-egg tier — cuttable without structural damage.
- **Sound sourcing is real work**: every clip must be original or openly licensed
  (§10). No sampling shortcuts exist without IP risk.
- **A day-one visitor cost:** the power-press entry adds one deliberate click before
  anything moves. Accepted as intent-capture and the audio-unlock gesture; the skip
  affordance serves returning visitors.
- All Lens-era visual documentation (ADR-005…011) becomes historical. Their process
  lessons (opt-in fidelity, subscribe-before-claim, owner-calibration-not-agent-guess)
  are carried forward explicitly in §8 and the plan.
