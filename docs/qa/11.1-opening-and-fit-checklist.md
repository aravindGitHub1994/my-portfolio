## §0 · Proved offline — do not re-run these

Everything here was measured against the **live** modules through `jiti`, not against
a copy. The harness is not committed (no harness in this repo ever has been); the
recipe is in `headless-qa-notes.md` under "project a candidate camera pose through the
real room, with an occlusion test", and it takes about an hour to rebuild.

**The viewport matrix used throughout** — thirteen entries, which closes the
phone-portrait-only hole that produced the iPad defect in the first place:
360×640 · 390×844 · 844×390 · 768×1024 · 1024×768 · 810×1080 · 1080×810 ·
820×1180 · 1180×820 · **1024×1366** · 1366×1024 · 1440×900 · 1920×1080.

### 0.1 · The reported defect, and that it is gone

| | old macro key | shipped key |
|---|---|---|
| power reach, frames breaching the 0.1 m near plane | **115 / 143** | **0 / 143** |
| worst cut | `forearmR` at −0.244 m | arm never enters the near region |
| nearest thing in shot, whole chapter | 0.063 m (`handL`) | **0.199 m** |

The mechanism was the near plane, not the hand: the arm meshes are `FrontSide`, so a
capsule cut at the lens shows no backface at all and the visitor looks straight
through into the figure. Verified at four aspects.

### 0.2 · Chapter 0's four tests, at all eight gate viewports

- **T1 occlusion** — the sight line to `POWER_WORLD` lands on the `towerPower` mesh
  first at every viewport; the anchor sits at NDC (−0.001, −0.085) landscape and
  (−0.001, −0.052) portrait.
- **T2 sizes** — the whole figure is uncropped at every viewport and reads 21 % × 63 %
  of frame at 1920×1080 (30 % × 38 % at portrait's 75° lens). Desk 34 % × 48 %.
- **T3 keep-out** — 0.146 m minimum clearance of the upper-body column, over the real
  `sampleCameraPath`.
- **T4 near plane** — clear at every viewport; nearest in shot 0.199–0.324 m.

### 0.3 · Three things about the shot that are forced, not chosen

Recorded because none of them is recoverable from the coordinates:

1. **Behind-*right*, because the figure occludes its own tower.** 438 of 972 swept
   camera positions were blocked by `chest` or `shoulderPivotR`. `PowerButtonAnchor`
   is projection-only with no depth test, so an occluded button leaves the entry
   ring floating over nothing — and the ring is the page's only affordance.
2. **The arc key is required.** A straight run from the opening to the rest clears
   the figure by **0.082 m at best and 0.024 m at the median** over the whole opening
   grid. Its `p` is at 83 % because that is the leg's share of the path length, so
   the two legs run at the same speed.
3. **The rest is on the figure's left.** The CRT sits at x −0.22 and the figure at
   x 0, so sight-line obliquity on the glass is **10° from the left, 37° from centre,
   46° from the right** — and a square-on medium wants exactly the space the figure
   occupies.

Also worth not re-deriving: plan-0011 §2.2's own suggested starting position
(1.25, 1.90, 1.40) **crops the figure** at some viewports.

### 0.4 · POST legibility (§2.3)

Floor = chapter 1's rest, which is where the lines used to still read. The new
chapter-0 rest clears it by **1.55×** at every one of the eight viewports — 7.02 px
of cap height against 4.54 px at 1920×1080 — while the screen stays **42 % of frame
height**, a medium and not a close-up. The floor and "still a medium" did not
conflict, so no shot decision is owed to the owner here.

  worst Δ **0.00e+0** over 20001 samples. (Poses *between* `REST_POINTS[0]` and
  `REST_POINTS[1]` do move — that segment's start key is the one ADR-014 §2 moved.)
- The desktop ride is bit-identical to `main` over 20001 progress samples at

- The shed ladder's offer lands at **34.6 / 32.5 / 38.4 s** at 10 / 20 / 27 fps —

- The dock rect fits both axes with non-negative `left`/`top` at all thirteen

### 0.6 · The title card (§3.2), timing and geometry

Measured from the press, at `POST_MS` 2740 ms: controls gone at 700 ms, card starts
fading at 603 ms, **card fully transparent at 2110 ms**, splash at 3290 ms. The
constraint is stated as `TITLE_HOLD_MS + TITLE_FADE_MS ≤ POST_MS`, against the POST
phase alone and deliberately ignoring the ~0.55 s of arm reach — so "the splash is
uncovered" holds however long the reach takes, including the watchdog path.

The card clears the projected ring at all thirteen viewports; tightest gap **48 px**,
at 844×390 phone landscape.

**Contrast over the real backdrop, measured** (ADR-014 §9 asks for measured, not
assumed). Method: screenshot the entry frame at 1440×900 twice, once with the two
`<p>`s `visibility: hidden`; diffing the pair gives the exact glyph pixels — much
tighter than the `<p>`'s bounding rect, which is full-width because the element is
block-level — then read the backdrop's luminance under exactly those pixels.

| line | glyph px | worst | median | share < 4.5:1 | share < 3:1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SITE.name` | 5308 | **9.69:1** | 15.18:1 | 0.00 % | 0.00 % |
| `SITE.role` | 1995 | **4.06:1** | 6.77:1 | 4.96 % | 0.00 % |

`SITE.name` clears AA on every pixel. `SITE.role` clears AA on 95 % of its pixels and
AA-large (3:1) on all of them. **The 99 sub-threshold pixels are all at x 529–541** —
the first characters of the line, sitting over the corner lamp's bloom. That is
precisely the interaction §9 named in advance ("the window shaft and P4's lamp are
both behind this text"), and it is why this measurement waited for P4.

Two things this number does **not** include, both of which only help: the
`[text-shadow:0_1px_3px_rgba(0,0,0,0.9)]` both lines carry, and the fact that the card
is dissolving from 603 ms onward rather than sitting there. Taken to the owner at §2
rather than pre-emptively retuned — the lamp's brightness is itself a §4 question.

### 0.7 · The lamp (§4.2) and the mug (§5.1)

- Brightness contract intact: `LUMINANCE_CAP 0.7`, `CAST_MAX 2.6`, and neither
  expression gains a lamp term. The lamp is **not** a shed rung.
- At `duskDeepen = 1` the lamp is at 0.450 against the CRT cast's **floor** of 0.500,
  so the screen is the last light standing even with a black screen.
- The bulb's linear emissive luminance is 1.179 against the tower LED's 1.018 — that
  LED is the one emissive in this scene proved to bloom through this pipeline. The
  shade sits at 0.306 and does not bloom.
- Lamp geometry: 20 mm off both walls, 24 mm off both baseboards, no bbox overlap
  with any prop including the cat tree.
- The coffee is the first raycast hit from the chapter-2 pose, the chapter-3 pose and
  straight down. Wisps are born 2 mm above the liquid, 4 mm below the rim, and a
  worst-case wisp clears the wall by 9.7 mm at rest and 7.6 mm at `MAX_TILT`.

### 0.8 · The wag (§6.1)

## Deliberate non-fixes, and things not claimed

1. **No `<h1>` in the accessibility tree while the experience is mounted.**
   `Hero.tsx:51` holds the page's only `<h1>` and it sits inside `[data-floor]`,
   which `globals.css` sets to `display: none` for capable clients. The prerendered
   HTML still ships it, so SEO and the no-JS floor are fine — but a screen-reader
   user riding the experience gets no page heading at all. **Pre-existing**, unrelated
   to this branch, and deliberately not fixed by §3.2: a heading that unmounts when
   the boot ends would be worse than none. It wants its own decision.
2. **iOS Safari's dynamic viewport is unhandled.** The runway is `750vh` while
   ScrollTrigger measures `innerHeight`, and nothing in the repo uses `dvh`/`svh` or
   `visualViewport`; every overlay is pinned in `vh`. If §1 finds progress jumping,
   that is a second defect with its own slice.
3. **The title card's role line dips under AA on 5 % of its pixels** — 4.06:1 at
   worst against 4.5:1, all of it where the line's first characters cross the corner
   lamp's bloom, and all of it still above AA-large's 3:1. Measured, not assumed
   (§0.6), and deliberately not retuned here: the fix is either the lamp's brightness
   (a §4 question) or the card's position (a §2 question), and both belong to the
   owner. The card is `aria-hidden` decoration duplicating content that lives in the
   DOM floor, so nothing is unreachable either way.
4. **`w98-intro-seen` is now write-only.** Nothing branches on it since the returning
   visitor's link was deleted. Kept as the page's only record that the intro has been
   seen; every checklist in this directory still opens by clearing it, and that step
   is now a no-op.
5. **ADR-013 §7a has a factual error.** It says two garnish rungs walk before the
   30 s deadline at 10 and 27 fps. Simulated against `main`'s own ten-rung module it
   is one. Recorded in `fidelity.ts` rather than silently corrected, because the
   number that matters — the offer's arrival time — did not move.
6. **No agent can judge a shot.** §0.2's four tests turn four properties of the
   opening into numbers, and every one of them can pass on a frame that is simply
   bad. §2 is the real acceptance, and plan-0011 risk 1 budgets for a second pass.
