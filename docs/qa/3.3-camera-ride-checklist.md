# Gate 3.3 — Whole-ride camera gate (owner)

> **Scope:** one uninterrupted pass of the camera journey, **forwards then
> backwards**, on a production static export. This is the gate for
> plan-0010's P3 (the camera route), but the ride now also carries P4 and
> P5.1, so anything that looks wrong is in scope even if it is not the
> camera.
>
> **What this gate is *not*:** the dock's full regression sweep (slice 8.1
> owns that, and it is the branch's biggest known risk), mobile, or audio
> levels. Note anything you see, but those have their own sittings.
>
> **This gate is yours.** Nothing in §1–§4 was passed autonomously — the
> agent verified geometry and framing numerically and explicitly could not
> judge how any of it *feels*. Fill in verdicts and notes as you go; the
> next agent session reads this file.
>
> **Verdict key:** `[x] Pass` · `[!] Needs change` · `[?] Unsure / discuss`

## 0 · Setup

Static export — `npm start` will not serve it correctly.

```bash
npm run build
npx serve out -l 3005      # → http://localhost:3005
```

Reset persisted state before the first-run pass. DevTools → Console:

```js
localStorage.removeItem("w98-intro-seen");     // skips the power-on intro
localStorage.removeItem("w98-muted");          // remembers your mute choice
localStorage.removeItem("w98-fidelity-floor"); // remembers an accepted downgrade
location.reload();
```

**Unmute.** Chapter 2 has an audio question in it this time (§4.1).

**Stripped from production builds** — `window.__fidelity`, `window.__armPose`,
`window.__sipNow`, and the on-screen perf counter. They exist only under
`npm run dev`. §3.3 is the one step that wants dev; everything else should be
judged on the real build.

**Reading position.** `window.__experienceState` gives `scrollProgress`,
`chapterIndex` and `docked`. To re-check one frame after the main pass:

```js
const goto = p => scrollTo(0, p * (document.documentElement.scrollHeight - innerHeight));
goto(0.467);   // chapter 2
```

Rest points: **0.12 · 0.28 · 0.467 · 0.653 · 0.813 · 1.0**.
Jumping past **0.813** engages the dock and suspends scroll — wheel a few
notches to undock before carrying on.

## 1 · The ride, forwards — the gate itself

Do this **once, in one sitting, without jumping**. Wheel or trackpad only.
The point is the joins, and jumping hides them.

- [ ] **Boot.** Click the glowing button. The arm reaches, the fingertip
      lands, *then* the machine wakes — sound on the fingertip, not the
      click. Camera holds a beat on the lit LED, then pans onto the glass
      while the POST runs. Scroll is parked throughout but the page does
      move ~700 px; that is the pan, not a stray scrub.
- [ ] **Ch 0 → 1** (`0.12 → 0.28`) — power-button macro out to the CRT
      close-up. No jerk at the segment boundary.
- [ ] **Ch 1 → 2** (`0.28 → 0.467`) — pull back off the glass and orbit onto
      the man. **Changed this session (3.1)** — see §2.1.
- [ ] **Ch 2 → 3** (`0.467 → 0.653`) — the arc out to the wide. **Changed
      this session (3.2)** — see §2.2. It should crane up and pull back on
      *one* side of the figure. Watch for it clipping through hair or
      shoulder; that is the failure this arc exists to avoid.
- [ ] **Ch 3 → 4** (`0.653 → 0.813`) — swing in over the shoulder and push
      square-on until the screen fills frame and the dock takes over.
- [ ] **Ch 4 → 5** (`0.813 → 1.0`) — undock and pull back to the widest;
      dusk has deepened.
- [ ] **The whole thing reads as one continuous move**, not six shots
      stitched together.

## 2 · The two shots that changed

### 2.1 · Chapter 2 — the face reveal (slice 3.1) · `goto(0.467)`

Was a hair *past* profile (mostly the back of a head). Now a three-quarter
from above-left, 40° off his facing axis.

- [ ] You read **beard, nose, hoop, earbud and curls**.
- [ ] **The missing eyes are not conspicuous.** `buildHead.ts` has no eyes and
      no mouth by decision — the eye zone is meant to sit in shadow under the
      brow, lit from below by the CRT. If it reads as a blank face rather than
      a shadowed one, that is a fail and it is the whole risk of the slice.
- [ ] The **tattooed right forearm** still reads in the shot.
- [ ] The **high angle** is acceptable. The camera looks down ~13°, and this
      is forced: he sits at a desk with a monitor in front of him, so there is
      no frontal angle at head height that keeps the CRT out of frame. If a
      downward angle feels wrong for this beat, say so — the fix is a
      different shot, not a nudge.

**Notes:**

### 2.2 · Chapter 3 — the room (slices 3.2 + 5.1) · `goto(0.653)`

The wide was composed *away* from the window wall; the glass, the shafts and
the (new) cat tree were all behind the camera. It now looks across the room
from the front-left.

- [ ] **Both cats are legible** and read as cats.
- [ ] They look like **Nimbus and Ivy** — the ginger and the grey-and-cream.

- [ ] **Light shafts and dust still read** — you are now looking toward the

- [ ] Nothing intersects the window frame, the sill or the wall.

## 3 · The rest of the scene, as seen from the ride

### 3.1 · The mug sip (slice 4.2)

The figure lifts the mug, drinks, sets it down. On a seeded 11–30 s gap with
roughly a 3-in-10 chance, so you may need to sit at chapter 2 or 3 for a
minute. **The tilt and head dip are already owner-approved — not under review
here.** What is:

- [ ] The mug returns to exactly its starting spot. No creep over several
      sips.
- [ ] Nothing snaps at the start or end of the reach.

### 3.2 · Mug steam (slice 4.3)

- [ ] Reads at dusk **without blowing out** — it should be heat off coffee,
      not smoke off a fire.
- [ ] **Rise speed.** This is the constant most likely to want changing and
      nobody has given a view on it. Too fast reads as smoke.
- [ ] It stays put when you scrub past it (a culling bug here was fixed this
      session; steam used to vanish when the camera got close).

### 3.3 · Optional, on `npm run dev` only

`window.__sipNow()` starts a sip immediately; `__sipNow(5)` holds it at his
mouth for 5 s. Worth it if you want to study the sip or the steam trail
without waiting for the scheduler.

## 4 · Questions I need you to answer

These are open calls, not defects. The agent could not settle any of them.

- [ ] **4.1 — The earbud leak at chapter 2.** 3.1 moved the camera from
      1.28 m to 0.98 m from his head, and the leak's falloff saturates at
      1.0 m — so the leak now plays at **full** at the chapter-2 rest, where
      it used to sit at ~72 %. Arguably right (closest approach = loudest
      glimpse inside his head) and no code changed, but no human has ever
      heard this mix. **Too loud, right, or not noticeable?**
- [ ] **4.2 — The boot pan's hold and travel.** How long the camera sits on
      the lit LED before panning to the glass. Raised at gate 2.4 and never
      answered individually; the pass covered it implicitly.
- [ ] **4.3 — Should the boot skip announce itself?** Any key or click skips
      the intro today, and it is deliberately undiscoverable. Still unresolved.
- [ ] **4.4 — Is 90 vh the right amount of *camera move* for chapter 0?**
      The pan eats that whole span, so it is no longer a question about
      scroll distance.
- [ ] **4.5 — The fidelity ladder arrives later on slower hardware.**
      Measured: 70 s to the static-floor offer at a pinned 20 fps, but
      **113 s at 10 fps**, because the grace between rungs counts frames, not
      seconds. The device that most needs the 2D floor waits longest for it.
      Leave it, or make grace time-based?

## 5 · The backwards pass

The camera path is a pure function of progress, so reverse scrubbing *should*
be artifact-free by construction. Confirm it actually is.

- [ ] Scrub from 1.0 back to 0 in one pass. Same six shots, no jerk, no pop.
- [ ] **Undocking works** — from the dock, scrolling up releases and the
      camera pulls back out. It must not need a timer or a second gesture.
- [ ] The scroll cue reappears where it should and stays clear of the
      sign-off links.
- [ ] Nothing is stuck: `__experienceState.docked` is `false` once you are
      past chapter 4 going backwards.

## 6 · Known and accepted — do **not** file these

Recorded so you can ignore them rather than re-report them:

- Single-`scale` window drag has ~5 % x error.
- Keyboard undock can leave progress a few thousandths past the rest point.
- The light-shaft billboard looks pale edge-on.
- The chair backrest is plain boxes.
- Faint moiré on the CRT at some angles.
- The figure's face has **no eyes and no mouth**. This is a decision, not an
  omission (ADR-012 §2 / ADR-013 §4). §2.1 asks whether it *reads*, which is
  a different question.
- Audio levels generally, mobile, and the fidelity ladder's pacing all have
  their own open threads and are not this gate.
